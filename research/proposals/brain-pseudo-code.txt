Short Concept Summary (100 Words):
"Liquid Synapse" transforms the abstract inner workings of the human brain into an 
interactive, tactile modular synthesizer and live audiovisual performance. Drawing from 
research in continuous-time "Liquid Neural Networks" and tactile hardware interface design, 
this two-part program allows museum visitors to physically sculpt cognitive state-spaces. 
In a hands-on workshop, participants patch virtual and physical control voltages to modulate 
neural ordinary differential equations (ODEs), turning EEG rhythms and attention dynamics into 
resonant acoustic timbres. In an evening performance in the Lee Exchange, these bio-modular 
instruments drive a massive continuous audiovisual projection on the two-story screen.

Key Questions Explored:
1. What does neural computation feel and sound like when freed from discrete tokens?
2. How can tangible hardware interfaces demystify brain-computer interfacing (BCI) for the public?
3. How do continuous dynamical systems bridge biological cognition and artificial intelligence?
